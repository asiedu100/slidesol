How to measure at home

  1. Stand on a sheet of paper against a wall, heel touching the wall.
  2. Mark the tip of your longest toe, then measure the distance from the wall to the mark in centimetres.
  3. Measure both feet — most people have one slightly larger foot — and size to the larger measurement.
